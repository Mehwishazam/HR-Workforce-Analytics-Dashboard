This HR Workforce Analytics dashboard presents a clear and well-rounded view of the organization’s workforce by combining key HR metrics with deeper attrition and demographic insights.
It covers total and active employees, attrition count, attrition rate, and average employee age to reflect overall workforce health.
The dashboard explores workforce distribution across departments, education fields, age groups, and gender, while also highlighting attrition patterns over time. Job satisfaction analysis by role adds further context to understand potential retention drivers.
With interactive filters and clear visual storytelling, this dashboard is designed to support data-driven HR decision-making and effective workforce planning.
